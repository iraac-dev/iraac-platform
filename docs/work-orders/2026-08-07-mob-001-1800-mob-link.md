## Goal

Prepare IRAAC for Phase 7/R9: the Location-based Aboriginal Service Connector
and 1800 Mob Link follow-up engine. The first release is an Illawarra-first
service-navigation, referral handoff and longitudinal outcome-follow-up
backbone that can later expand nationally.

The program should let Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander community members
log in, search by location, see local and national services, request support,
receive safe connection to local supports, and consent to later call-centre
follow-up that checks whether those supports actually helped.

## Non-goals

- Do not launch a public phone number from this work order.
- Do not import real community contacts, staff contacts, service-client data,
  recordings, transcripts or referral notes.
- Do not represent the service as emergency response, legal advice, counselling
  or a replacement for 000, 13YARN, ALS, health services or specialist crisis
  services.
- Do not switch from Supabase to Convex without a separate architecture
  decision.
- Do not put real community phone numbers into any external provider before
  privacy, data-location, consent and SMS-subprocessor review.
- Do not publish named service-performance findings without legal,
  cultural-governance and publication approval.

## Acceptance tests

- The roadmap names the Service Connector + 1800 Mob Link as a strategic
  Phase 7/R9 program after the existing Listen, Reports, Email, SMS/human phone
  and AI-voice gates.
- The implementation plan preserves consent, suppression, privacy, Indigenous
  Data Sovereignty, audit, small-cell reporting, human escalation and
  no-real-outreach boundaries.
- Supabase Auth is selected for community login and database enforcement.
- The service-directory design separates public service information from
  private referral details.
- Intake, referral and follow-up are modelled as longitudinal records that can
  report outcomes without exposing unsafe personal detail.
- Supabase remains the system of record unless a future ADR proves a different
  backend is safer.
- A testing branch can be pushed to GitHub without force-push or direct `main`
  work.

## Human decisions

- Confirm the final 1800 Mob Link name and number reservation path.
- Decide whether the pilot is adult-only, youth-inclusive, or youth-specific,
  and obtain legal and child-safety review before any youth flow.
- Approve crisis-routing scripts, legal-service disclaimers, mandatory
  reporting thresholds, domestic and family violence safety language, callback
  limits and human escalation rules.
- Approve the Illawarra service-directory seed list, maintenance owner,
  service right-of-reply process and government reporting audience.
- Approve Supabase Auth community login, phone OTP, AI call, call recording,
  transcript retention, map provider, SMS/email provider or service-portal
  account design.
